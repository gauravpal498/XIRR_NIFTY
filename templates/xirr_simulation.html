NIFTY 50: buying when P/E < 20 vs a monthly SIP

XIRR summary

Each simulation picks a random window (length uniform between min and max, start uniform within the data). The P/E strategy buys ₹100 at the close on every trading day in the window with P/E below the threshold. The SIP runs 28 times per window, once for each day of the month from 1 to 28. It buys ₹100 on that day each month, or on the next trading day when that day is a holiday. Both are valued at the last close in the window. Windows with no P/E-below-threshold day are discarded for both strategies. XIRR is annualised, so very short windows produce extreme values.
